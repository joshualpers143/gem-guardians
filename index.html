📲 Install & Game Information

Everything about installing, updating, offline play, and keeping your progress. This guide is separate from Update Info.

How to Install Gem Guardians on Android (Brave / Chrome)

  • Public game website: https://joshualpers143.github.io/gem-guardians/. Open this HTTPS link on your Android phone in Brave or Google Chrome, and wait for the game to load.
  • In Brave: tap the three-dot menu (⋮), choose Install app or Add to Home screen, and confirm. In Chrome: tap the three-dot menu (⋮), choose Install app or Add to Home screen, and confirm. The exact wording may vary by browser and device.
  • Find the Gem Guardians icon on your home screen or in your app drawer, then tap it to play. If you only see Add to Home screen, it may create a shortcut instead of a standalone app.
  • After a successful first online load and caching, the game can be played offline. Open the game online after an update so the latest files can download before going offline.
  • To publish future Public Edition updates, upload the updated website files to the same GitHub repository (gem-guardians), replacing the existing files without changing the website address or the saved-data key. Do not upload Creator Edition files to the public repository.
  • Progress automatically saves locally in the current browser or installed app every five seconds and on app hide/exit; you can also press Save. Test by making progress, pressing Save, reopening the game and checking that progress remains.
  • Old downloaded HTML files, Brave, Chrome, other phones, and the Creator Edition may use different storage. Saves do not automatically sync or transfer between them. Keep old files, and do not clear browser/site data or uninstall the app if you need its local progress.
  • If Install app is missing, try Add to Home screen, reload the HTTPS page, or open the link in Chrome. An installed shortcut does not necessarily guarantee full offline/PWA behavior.

Important: Installing Future Game Updates & Keeping Your Progress

  • Every new Gem Guardians update must be loaded to receive its new features and fixes. Open your installed game while connected to the internet, then close and reopen it if needed to activate the latest update. You normally do not need to uninstall or reinstall the app.
  • Your game progress is designed to stay saved across updates on the same installed game and browser, including your units, gems, levels, and other progression. Updates preserve the same website address and save keys.
  • Important: Progress is saved locally on your device, not in an online account. Clearing browser/app data, uninstalling, changing browsers or devices, or a storage failure may erase it. Updates cannot guarantee recovery from those events.
  • Always use the official Public Edition website: https://joshualpers143.github.io/gem-guardians/. Do not download a separate HTML file for each update.
  • If a new feature does not appear, connect to the internet, open the game website, refresh once, then fully close and reopen the installed game. Avoid clearing site data, as this may delete progress.

Installable Offline PWA & Stable Saves

  • Install Gem Guardians from a stable HTTPS web address; game code is cached for offline play after the first visit.
  • Progress is saved to this edition's browser storage automatically every five seconds, on app hide/exit, and when Save is pressed. The previous valid save is retained as a local backup.
  • Future releases must replace the files at the exact same HTTPS origin and deployment path and keep the save key unchanged (gemGuardiansSave); save migration reads older supported fields without wiping progression.
  • Creator and Public are separate installations and save keys; neither accesses the other's data.
  • Opening a previously downloaded local HTML file uses a different storage context. Its save cannot automatically migrate into this PWA. Do not delete old files or browser data until your old progress is recovered.
  • Offline saves are local to the browser/app; uninstalling, clearing site data or switching browsers/devices can erase or hide progress. This is not cloud sync.

New Feature

Unit Abilities: Every Unit Plays Differently

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 12:37 AM MDT

  • Real abilities for 50 units. Every Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary unit now has one named ability that does exactly what its card says. Before, many cards only said a word like "burn" or "swarm", and units shared hidden bonuses that were never explained.
  • New effects on enemies: Burn and Poison deal damage over time (Poison stacks up to 3 times), Sunder makes an enemy take more damage from every unit, Root and Stun stop it, Freeze locks it in place, and Knockback pushes it back down the road. Small icons under each enemy show what is affecting it.
  • Counters for every enemy: Squire and Light Warden smash Crystal Shellback shields; Falconer, Wind Scout and Archer hit Gloomwings harder; burning stops Moss Shamans from healing well; Moss Druid roots stop Tide Crabs from diving; Forest Hunter finishes off weak enemies; splash and chain units clear Gloop pieces.
  • Support units: Wandering Monk makes nearby units attack 10% faster, Dawn Paladin Blesses nearby units with +12% damage, and Star Oracle, Seraph Sentinel, Hex Witch and Alchemist Sunder enemies for the whole team. Iron Miner digs up coins.
  • Role tags: tap any unit to see its ability with tags such as Area, Control, Support, Anti-Shield, Anti-Air, Finisher and Economy, so building a balanced team is easier.
  • Mythic and Secret unit cards now describe exactly what their abilities do. Their power is unchanged.
  • The Help screen explains each ability effect icon.
Bug Fix

Trait Fixes & Rebalance

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 10:02 PM MDT

  • Auto Reroll no longer throws away great traits. Before, it paused for only 3 seconds on a Legendary or Mythic and then rolled over it, and when hunting a target it skipped other Mythics. Now you choose when it stops: Legendary or better (default), Mythic only, or only my target trait.
  • Gem reserve: Auto Reroll stops before spending below the amount you set (500 gems by default) instead of using every gem.
  • Safety check: rerolling a unit that has a Legendary or Mythic trait, or starting Auto Reroll on it, now asks you first.
  • Odds shown: the Trait Forge lists the chance of each rarity (Common 40%, Rare 30%, Epic 20%, Legendary 9%, Mythic 1%).
  • Trait rarities now use the same names as units: the old Uncommon traits are now Rare, and the old Rare traits are now Epic. Their effects did not change.
  • Balance: Mythic traits were far stronger than everything else (about 2.6–2.75× damage per second). They now give about 2×: Astral Overlord +60% damage, +20% range, +25% speed; Singularity +35% damage, +50% range, +45% speed; Cosmic Ascendant +80% damage, +10% range, +10% speed. Legendary traits are slightly stronger: War Monarch +50% damage, +12% speed; Far Horizon +25% damage, +40% range, +18% speed. Midas is unchanged. Units keep the traits they have.

Player EXP Rebalance

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 9:36 PM MDT

Rarer Units Need More EXP

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 03:18 PM MDT

  • Stronger units now take longer to level up. Each level needs more EXP the rarer the unit is: Common ×1, Rare ×1.15, Epic ×1.35, Exclusive ×1.35, Legendary ×1.6, Mythic ×1.9, Secret ×2.3.
  • This stacks with the prestige-star increase (+50% per star). For example, a level-1 Secret unit with 2★ needs about 333 EXP for its next level (72 × 2.3 × 2).
  • Total EXP from level 1 to 100 without stars: about 197k for Common, 266k for Epic, 315k for Legendary, 374k for Mythic and 452k for Secret.
  • Unit cards show the exact EXP each unit needs. Units keep their current levels, and EXP already earned toward the next level is kept.
Balance Update

Unit Leveling & Prestige Rebalanced

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 03:08 PM MDT

  • Units were leveling up far too fast: one win on a later map could jump a unit dozens of levels at once. Leveling is now much slower and steadier.
  • Every level needs an exact amount of EXP, shown on each unit card as EXP current / needed. It rises smoothly from 72 EXP at level 1 to about 5,200 EXP at level 99. Reaching level 100 from level 1 now takes about 197,000 EXP (it was about 43,000).
  • Battles and fruit give the same EXP as before, so a unit no longer levels up after every single battle, and high-level units take real effort.
  • Prestige stars raise the EXP needed: every star a unit has makes each level need 50% more EXP (1★ ×1.5, 2★ ×2, 3★ ×2.5, 4★ ×3, 5★ ×3.5). Prestiging is still worth it, because every star makes the unit permanently stronger.
  • Only Epic and rarer units can prestige. Common and Rare units are meant to be starter units, so they stop at level 100 without stars. The Prestige button only appears on Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret and Exclusive units.
  • Units keep the levels and prestige stars they already have, including any stars a Common or Rare unit earned before this update.
