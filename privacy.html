💎 Gem Guardians — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Short version: Gem Guardians does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress (units, gems, levels, settings and similar game data) is saved only on your own device, in the app’s local storage.
  • Sound, display and phone preferences are also stored only on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, logins, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

Backups you make

If you use Export, Share or Copy Save, a backup of your game data is created as a file or text by you. Where you keep or send that backup is your choice; the game does not upload it anywhere.

Internet use

The game works offline. When online, it only downloads its own game files to check for and install updates. No personal information is included in these requests.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Game Progress, or uninstall the app or clear its site data. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, this removes all of your game data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game’s GitHub page: github.com/joshualpers143/gem-guardians.
