💎 Gem Guardians — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Short version: Gem Guardians works fully offline and does not sell or share personal information. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to sign in to the optional Cloud Save.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your game progress (units, gems, levels, settings and similar game data) is saved on your own device, in the app’s local storage.
  • Sound, display and phone preferences are also stored only on your device.
  • There are no ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

Optional Cloud Save

Cloud Save is off unless you sign in under Settings → Cloud Save. If you sign in:

  • Your player name, which you choose. Player names are public: other players can see them (for example on future leaderboards), so do not use your real name or email as your player name.
  • Your email address and a password (stored only in protected, hashed form by our sign-in provider) are used to create your account.
  • A copy of your game progress, the game version and a short summary (player level, gems, number of units) are stored so you can restore them on another device.
  • This data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our sign-in and database. Each player can only access their own save.
  • We use this data only to provide Cloud Save. We never sell it, share it for advertising, or send marketing email.

Backups you make

If you use Export, Share or Copy Save, a backup of your game data is created as a file or text by you. Where you keep or send that backup is your choice.

Internet use

The game works offline. When online, it downloads its own game files to check for updates, and, only if you signed in to Cloud Save, it backs up and restores your save.

Children

Playing does not require an account. Cloud Save is optional; children should ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud account and cloud saves: Settings → Cloud Save → Delete cloud account permanently deletes your account, email and every cloud save. You can also ask for deletion through the contact link below.
  • Progress on your device: use Settings → Reset Game Progress, or uninstall the app or clear its site data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent through the game’s GitHub page: github.com/joshualpers143/gem-guardians.
