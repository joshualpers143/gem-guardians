New Feature

Raids Auto Play Pass

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 3:30 AM MDT

  • The raid pass now unlocks what it was meant to: Auto Play in Duskmoor Castle raids. The 🏰 Raids ×3 Speed pass is now the 🏰 Raids Auto Play pass, found in 💎 Passes. Anyone who owned it keeps it.
  • With the pass, the Tactician can play every raid level you have unlocked, and Auto Restart works in raids too.
  • Speed ×3 in raids works like everywhere else again: it comes with the ×3 Speed pass (and the free ×3 for early players). Raid leaderboard runs are timed the same way as map runs.
  • Auto Play on regular maps is unchanged and stays free (Player Level 5 and a 3-star clear).
