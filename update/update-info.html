Bug Fix

Summoned Creatures Fight by Collision

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 1:15 PM MDT

  • Every creature a unit summons onto the road now works the same way: Abyss Summoner's shadow monsters and Elite Ghouls, and Abyss Monarch's Hell Wolves.
  • They appear at your base and walk the road toward the enemy entrance. Hell Wolves used to start near the enemy entrance and run toward your base.
  • They no longer shoot. They damage enemies by running into them: shadow monsters 55%, Elite Ghouls 150% and Hell Wolves 125% of the unit's damage per collision. A creature can't walk through an enemy, so it keeps ramming the enemy in front of it (one hit every 0.6 seconds) until one of them falls. Enemies are not stopped by creatures.
  • Every creature has its own HP bar (teal). Its HP is 275% of the summoning unit's damage (damage +175%). Each boost gives the unit +35% damage, so its creatures get +35% HP too, including the ones already on the road.
  • Every collision costs the creature HP: 8% of that enemy's max HP (5% for bosses and mini-bosses). It bounces back a little after each hit and disappears when its HP runs out or it reaches the enemy entrance.
  • Submerged Tide Crabs can't be hit, so creatures pass over them.
