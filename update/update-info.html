New Feature

Battle Effects Overhaul

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 8:51 PM MDT

  • Hits you can feel: enemies flash white when hit, with sparks; heavy hits throw golden sparks.
  • Defeats: enemies burst in their own color with a fading spirit, and a coin flies to your coin counter, which pulses as it lands.
  • Wave banners: “WAVE 5” slides across the map at the start of each wave, and the last wave gets a gold “FINAL WAVE” banner.
  • Bosses: a red warning banner, a screen shake and a red flash when a boss or mini-boss arrives, a boss health bar at the top of the map, and a big golden explosion with a shower of coins when it falls.
  • Your units: a landing ring and dust when you place a unit; golden rings, sparkles and “BOOST 3” text when you boost one.
  • A red flash around the map when an enemy reaches your castle, and confetti when you win.
  • Screen shake can be turned off in Settings. Performance mode turns off the particles and shake but keeps the banners and boss bar.
