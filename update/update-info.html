Balance Update

Tougher Raids, Bigger Rewards

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 2:06 AM MDT

  • Duskmoor Castle raids were much too easy: their enemies used the first map's health. Raid enemies are now as tough as Volcanic Rift on Hell at Level 1 and grow +25% per raid level. Level 5 keeps its 30 waves and is the hardest challenge in the game.
  • Raid gem rewards are higher: 250 / 400 / 550 / 700 / 1,000 gems for Levels 1 to 5. Level 5 still has its 5% chance at Count Vesper.
  • Levels you already cleared stay cleared.
Bug Fix

Saves Are Protected From Cheating

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 2:06 AM MDT

  • Save files could be downloaded, edited by hand and loaded back in to fake progress. To keep the game fair and protect players from scams, Export, Share, Copy, Import and Paste Save are removed.
  • Your progress still saves automatically in the game's own storage on this device. Sign in to Cloud Save to keep a safe copy online and to continue on another phone.
  • If you restore your Cloud Save by mistake, Settings → Undo Last Cloud Restore brings back the progress you had before.
  • Passes and purchases are kept on the server with your account, never in the save, so they can't be faked.
