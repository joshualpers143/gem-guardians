New Feature

VIP Perks: More Gems and EXP

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 1:30 AM MDT

  • VIP now also gives +25% gems from battles, raids, Infinite waves, AFK and quests, +25% unit EXP and +50% player EXP, on top of the gold name, crown and golden glow.
  • The bonus is shown on the Victory/Defeat card as a gold 👑 VIP bonus line. Selling units, Index rewards and level milestone rewards are not boosted.
  • Every pass is permanent: once your account has it, you keep it forever, on every device through Cloud Save.
Balance Update

Speed ×3 in Raids Is Now Its Own Pass

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 1:30 AM MDT

  • Speed ×3 in Duskmoor Castle raids now needs the new 🏰 Raids ×3 Speed pass, found in 💎 Passes.
  • The ×3 Speed pass and the free ×3 for early players still cover every map battle and Infinite Mode, but not raids. Speed ×1 and ×2 stay free in raids for everyone.
  • Raid leaderboard runs are timed for Speed ×2 unless your account owns the Raids ×3 Speed pass.
