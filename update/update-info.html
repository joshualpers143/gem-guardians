3D Detail Pass: Real 3D Camera

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 4:35 PM MDT

  • Battles now use a real angled 3D camera with depth and perspective, distant hills and soft fog, and the whole battlefield always fits on screen.
  • Units and enemies are bigger and more detailed, and every placed unit stands on a stone platform with a glowing rune circle in its type colour.
  • Every map has lamp posts with warm light along the road, stone road edges and floating sparkles. The gem castle has round towers with cone roofs, battlements, lit windows and a banner.
  • Duskmoor Castle gets rounded and cross gravestones, iron fences and branching dead trees.
  • Tapping still places and selects units exactly where you tap, and health bars, levels, damage numbers and the boost panel follow the camera.
  • New Settings → 🎥 3D camera: Close (the default, a big battlefield), Cinematic (a low angle with sky, stars and a horizon) or Top-down (the classic view). Saved on this device.
