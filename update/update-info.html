New Feature

Detailed 3D Characters & Rare Pull Celebrations

Implemented Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at 8:45 AM MDT

  • Every unit was rebuilt with much more detail: real eyes with shine, eyebrows that match each unit’s mood, nose and mouth, hair with fringes and side locks, jointed arms and legs, boots, belts with buckles, collars, armour shoulder plates and capes that flow down the back.
  • Rarity shows on the model: Legendary units get glowing gold trims and a chest gem, and Mythic and Secret units have crystals floating around them.
  • Dragons, the Worldtree, the Phoenix, Xeno Warden and the serpent got new detail: horns, teeth, wing bones, claws, spines, roots, branches, feathers and glowing eyes.
  • Unit portraits everywhere are new: a rarity-coloured backdrop with light rays, a stone pedestal and coloured rim lighting.
  • The Rotating Summon Banner is redesigned: the featured Secret and Mythic are shown big at the top, then Legendary, Epic and Rare, then the Commons, with a countdown bar to the next banner.
  • Pulling a Mythic now shows a highlight: light rays, sparkles and the unit’s portrait, and its card glows in the results.
  • Pulling a Secret plays a full cinematic: the screen darkens, crimson lightning strikes, a shockwave and embers, a shake and the unit’s portrait.
  • Both also play during Auto Summon without pausing it. Turn them off in Settings → 🌟 Mythic & Secret pull effects.
