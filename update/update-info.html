New Feature

Cloud Save (Optional)

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 2:04 AM MDT

  • Back up your progress online. Open Settings → Cloud Save and create an account with your email and a password. Your progress then backs up automatically about once a minute while you play and are online, and you can tap Back up now any time.
  • Continue on another device. Sign in on another phone or on the website and tap Load cloud save.
  • Your saves are protected. If this device and the cloud have different progress, the game never overwrites either one by itself; it shows both (level, gems and units) and lets you choose which to keep. Loading a cloud save keeps a recovery copy of your previous progress.
  • Still fully offline. Cloud Save is optional. Without signing in, nothing leaves your device, exactly as before.
  • You can sign out at any time, or delete your cloud account and all its cloud saves from the same screen. The privacy policy has been updated to explain what Cloud Save stores.
