Faster Replies From the Creator

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 11:10 PM MDT

  • The Creator now gets an alert on their phone as soon as a support ticket needs them, and can answer it right from the game, so tickets the support helper can't solve get a reply sooner.
  • Support works through written messages in your ticket only. There are no phone, voice or video calls, and support will never ask for your phone number or other contact details.
