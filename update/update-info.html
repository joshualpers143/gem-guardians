New Feature

Secret Trait: Void Sovereign

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 9:46 AM MDT

  • ☠ Void Sovereign is the first Secret trait and the strongest in the game: +500% damage, +50% range and +30% attack speed.
  • The price of power: a unit with Void Sovereign can be placed only once per battle.
  • Every trait reroll has a 0.1% chance to roll it. Secret pity: 1,000 rerolls without it guarantees Void Sovereign. The Trait Forge shows your Secret pity.
  • An ominous look: a dark pool with a burning crimson sigil, rising shadow smoke, orbiting embers and glowing red eyes, plus dark-red hits in battle.
  • Rolling it plays a full-screen reveal. Auto Reroll always stops when it lands, and replacing it asks first.
  • New odds per reroll: Common 39.9%, Rare 30%, Epic 20%, Legendary 9%, Mythic 1%, Secret 0.1%.
New Feature

Auto Reroll Is Now a Pass

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 9:46 AM MDT

  • Auto Reroll in the Trait Forge is now the 🔁 Auto Reroll pass (💎 Passes in the ☰ menu).
  • Thank you, current players: if you played before this update, you keep Auto Reroll for free, and accounts that existed before this update have it too.
  • Rerolling by hand stays free for everyone, and nobody loses a trait they already have.
  • New: the developer can now send gifts to a player’s account. They arrive the next time you open the game signed in to Cloud Save.
