New Feature

Systems Unlock as You Level Up & New Tutorials

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 8:27 PM MDT

  • Easier start: new players begin with Summon, Battle, Units, Bag and Quests. Other systems unlock with Player Level: Index at 2, Traits at 3, AFK at 4, 5th team slot and Auto Play at 5, Upgrade Paths at 6, Prestige and the 6th slot at 10, Raids at 25. Locked tabs show a lock and the level they open at.
  • New beginner tutorial: 9 hands-on steps (summon, equip, place, upgrade, win) instead of 24.
  • A tutorial for every system: when something unlocks, a “New system unlocked!” card explains it and can walk you through it.
  • Help & Tutorials (in the ☰ menu) lets you replay any tutorial or read the game overview.
  • Before Upgrade Paths unlock, Boost 3 applies each unit’s recommended path automatically. Daily quests never ask for a locked system.
  • Players who already had progress keep every system unlocked (Raids still need Level 25).
