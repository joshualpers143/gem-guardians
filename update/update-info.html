New Feature

Visual Overhaul & Summon Reveal

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 6:07 PM MDT

  • New icon set: tabs, header buttons, battle badges and headings now use hand-drawn game icons instead of emoji, so they look the same on every phone.
  • Refreshed look: deeper background, glossy buttons that press down when tapped, rounded panels with soft shadows, and unit cards that glow in their rarity color.
  • Crystal Summon altar: the Summon tab has an animated crystal and two big summon buttons that show their gem cost.
  • Summon reveal: after a manual summon, your new units flip in as cards. Legendary, Mythic and Secret pulls get a colored light burst. Tap Continue, or Summon again. Auto Summon skips it, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • The gem counter shows thousands separators (for example 18,650).
  • New app icon: a faceted gem in a gold ring, used for the home-screen app and the Android app.
  • Performance mode and the phone’s reduce-motion setting stop the decorative animations.
