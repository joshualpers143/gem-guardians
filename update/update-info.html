New Feature

Leaderboards: Most Time Played & Most Summons

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 10:52 AM MDT

  • Two new tabs in 🏆 Leaderboards: ⏱️ Time played and 🎰 Summons.
  • Time played counts while the game is open on your screen and you are signed in with your player name. The game server keeps the time itself, and playing on two devices at once never counts double.
  • Summons counts every summon you make while signed in (a ×10 counts as 10), even offline. They are sent when you are back online.
  • Both boards started counting with this update, so everyone starts from zero.
  • Fair play: the server only accepts summons at a realistic speed and never more time than really passed.
