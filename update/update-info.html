New Feature

Unique Player Names

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 2:55 AM MDT

  • Every online player now has their own name. After signing in to Cloud Save, you choose a player name that nobody else can use. The game checks it live and shows ✓ when it is available.
  • No copycats: names that look the same count as the same name, so if "Josh" is taken, "JOSH", "josh" and "J0sh" are taken too.
  • Rules: 3–16 letters, numbers or _, starting with a letter. Rude words and names like "Admin" or "Moderator" are not allowed.
  • Names are permanent, so choose carefully; the game asks you to confirm first. Your name follows your account to every device.
  • Players who do not sign in keep their offline name, which only they can see.
