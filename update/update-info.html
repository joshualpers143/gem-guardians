Balance Update

Player EXP Rebalance

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 9:36 PM MDT

  • Harder difficulties pay off: Medium gives ×1.5 Player EXP, Hard ×2.5 and Hell ×4 (before, Hell gave only 10% more than Easy).
  • Later maps are worth more: +6% per map, so Map 12 gives about twice as much as Map 1 (before, +1.5% per map).
  • First-clear bonus: +60% the first time you win a map on each difficulty. Star bonus: +10% per star.
  • Losses count: a lost battle now gives 25% of the win EXP, so trying a hard map is never wasted.
  • Raids: 120 EXP on Level 1, up to 200 on Level 5 (was 50).
  • Example: Map 1 Easy with 3 stars on the first win gives 104 EXP (was 50). Map 12 Hell with 3 stars gives 494 (was 65). Reaching Level 25 for Raids now takes roughly 100 battles instead of about 420.
  • Your current level and EXP are kept.
