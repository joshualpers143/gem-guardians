New Feature

Contact Support

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 7:26 PM MDT

  • Need help? Open ☰ → 🛟 Support (or Help & Tutorials → Contact Support) and tap New ticket. Every ticket gets its own number, for example #1001.
  • Our support helper (an AI) reads your message and answers right away, usually within a few seconds. You can keep chatting in the same ticket.
  • If the helper can't solve it, it tells you, and your ticket goes to the Creator, who will help you when they're not busy. Their reply shows up in the same ticket.
  • A red badge on ☰ and 🛟 Support tells you when there's a new reply, even if you closed the window.
  • You can include basic game info (level, gems, units, version, device) so we can help faster. Please never share passwords or personal details.
  • Contacting support needs an internet connection and a Cloud Save sign-in, so we can send the reply to you. The game itself still works fully offline.
