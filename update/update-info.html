New Feature

Leaderboards

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 9:36 AM MDT

  • Compete with other players! Open 🏆 Leaderboards from the Battle screen, the Raids panel or the ☰ menu.
  • Infinite Mode: one leaderboard per map, ranked by the highest wave you reach.
  • Raids: ranked by the highest raid level you clear; faster clears rank higher. Raid levels count in order, starting from Level 1.
  • How to join: sign in to Cloud Save, claim your unique player name, and be online when a run starts. Your result appears on the battle result card, with your rank and a 🏆 when you set a new best.
  • Fair play: the server times every run itself, so results that are faster than the game allows (even at Speed ×3) are not counted.
  • If your run ends while you are offline, the result is sent automatically when you are back online (within a day).
