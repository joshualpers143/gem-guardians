New Feature

Battles in Full 3D

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 12:30 PM MDT

  • Every battle is now drawn in real 3D: all 12 maps and Duskmoor Castle are lit 3D scenes with terrain, roads, trees, crystals, lava, graves and more, an enemy entrance and your gem castle, with shadows, glow and soft bloom.
  • Every unit is a 3D model built from its look: outfits, hats, hair, weapons, shields, capes, wings and glowing eyes. Dragons, the Phoenix, the Worldtree and aliens have their own creature models. Prestige stars show as golden rings and sparkles.
  • Every enemy is a 3D model too (imps, beetles, goblins, void knights, demon bosses, shellbacks, gloomwings, shamans, gloops, crabs, lynxes and brutes), turning to face where they walk, with frozen and slowed tints.
  • Shots, hits, deaths, coins, fire and mud fields, wards and summons are glowing 3D effects that light up the ground around them.
  • Placing, tapping, ranges, health bars, levels and damage numbers work exactly as before.
  • Prefer the classic look or have a slower phone? Settings → 🧊 3D graphics switches back to 2D (saved on this device).
