New Feature

Game Passes

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 2:18 PM MDT

  • New 💎 Passes window in the ☰ menu. Passes are one-time unlocks saved to your Cloud Save account, so they work on every device. They add convenience or looks, never extra power.
  • ⏩ ×3 Speed: Speed ×1 and ×2 stay free for everyone; Speed ×3 needs the pass.
  • 🌙 2× AFK Rewards: double gems (10–20 a minute) and a 10% trait reroll chance in AFK mode.
  • 🎰 Auto Summon+: Auto Summon starts with 450 gems (instead of more than 1,000) and keeps waiting for gems instead of stopping below 400.
  • 👑 VIP: gold name and crown in the header, profile and leaderboards, and a golden glow under your units.
  • Thank you, early players: if you played before this update, you keep ×3 Speed for free. Accounts that existed before this update have it on the leaderboards too.
  • 🎟️ Redeem codes: passes can be unlocked with codes from events and gifts (sign in to Cloud Save). Buying passes is coming soon.
  • Leaderboard fairness: ranked runs at Speed ×3 need the pass on your account.
