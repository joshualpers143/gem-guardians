New Look for Game Passes

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 4:30 AM MDT

  • The 💎 Game Passes window has a fresh design: VIP is a golden featured card at the top, and every other pass has its own colored card.
  • Each pass now shows a short tagline and clear "what you get" points instead of a long paragraph.
  • A progress bar shows how many passes your account owns, and owned passes get a glowing border and an OWNED ribbon.
  • On wider screens the passes sit side by side; on phones they stack.
