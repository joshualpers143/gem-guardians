Bug Fix

Trait Fixes & Rebalance

Implemented Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 10:02 PM MDT

  • Auto Reroll no longer throws away great traits. Before, it paused for only 3 seconds on a Legendary or Mythic and then rolled over it, and when hunting a target it skipped other Mythics. Now you choose when it stops: Legendary or better (default), Mythic only, or only my target trait.
  • Gem reserve: Auto Reroll stops before spending below the amount you set (500 gems by default) instead of using every gem.
  • Safety check: rerolling a unit that has a Legendary or Mythic trait, or starting Auto Reroll on it, now asks you first.
  • Odds shown: the Trait Forge lists the chance of each rarity (Common 40%, Rare 30%, Epic 20%, Legendary 9%, Mythic 1%).
  • Trait rarities now use the same names as units: the old Uncommon traits are now Rare, and the old Rare traits are now Epic. Their effects did not change.
  • Balance: Mythic traits were far stronger than everything else (about 2.6–2.75× damage per second). They now give about 2×: Astral Overlord +60% damage, +20% range, +25% speed; Singularity +35% damage, +50% range, +45% speed; Cosmic Ascendant +80% damage, +10% range, +10% speed. Legendary traits are slightly stronger: War Monarch +50% damage, +12% speed; Far Horizon +25% damage, +40% range, +18% speed. Midas is unchanged. Units keep the traits they have.
