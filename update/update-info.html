Balance Update

Faster Raids

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 4:00 AM MDT

  • Raids took too long: Raid Level 5 could take over 11 minutes even at Speed ×3. Enemies in raids now arrive almost twice as fast (every 0.4s instead of 0.75s) and the next wave starts after 3 seconds instead of 5.
  • Raid Level 5 now takes about 5 to 6 minutes at Speed ×3, and Levels 1 to 4 under 2 minutes. Waves, rewards and the Count Vesper chance are unchanged.
  • Because enemies now come in denser waves, their health was lowered to keep raids exactly as hard as before.
  • Raid leaderboard times are checked with the new pace. Regular maps are unchanged.
QoL

Skill Tags for Every Unit

Implemented Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 4:00 AM MDT

  • Every unit's Skills & Passives now shows its ability name and tags like Single Target, Area or Control. Count Vesper, Barter, Chrono Warden and Abyss Summoner were missing them.
  • New tag: Summoner, for units that send out their own fighters.
