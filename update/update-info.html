New Feature

Unit Abilities: Every Unit Plays Differently

Implemented Monday, October 12, 2026 at 12:37 AM MDT

  • Real abilities for 50 units. Every Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary unit now has one named ability that does exactly what its card says. Before, many cards only said a word like "burn" or "swarm", and units shared hidden bonuses that were never explained.
  • New effects on enemies: Burn and Poison deal damage over time (Poison stacks up to 3 times), Sunder makes an enemy take more damage from every unit, Root and Stun stop it, Freeze locks it in place, and Knockback pushes it back down the road. Small icons under each enemy show what is affecting it.
  • Counters for every enemy: Squire and Light Warden smash Crystal Shellback shields; Falconer, Wind Scout and Archer hit Gloomwings harder; burning stops Moss Shamans from healing well; Moss Druid roots stop Tide Crabs from diving; Forest Hunter finishes off weak enemies; splash and chain units clear Gloop pieces.
  • Support units: Wandering Monk makes nearby units attack 10% faster, Dawn Paladin Blesses nearby units with +12% damage, and Star Oracle, Seraph Sentinel, Hex Witch and Alchemist Sunder enemies for the whole team. Iron Miner digs up coins.
  • Role tags: tap any unit to see its ability with tags such as Area, Control, Support, Anti-Shield, Anti-Air, Finisher and Economy, so building a balanced team is easier.
  • Mythic and Secret unit cards now describe exactly what their abilities do. Their power is unchanged.
  • The Help screen explains each ability effect icon.
