QoL

Easier Scrolling & Quick Feed

Implemented Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 8:35 AM MDT

  • No more stuck scrolling: your unit collection, the Bag and the Index used to sit inside their own scroll boxes. On phones, a swipe inside them could not scroll the page, so it was hard to get back up. They now scroll with the page.
  • Small unit pickers (Traits) still scroll, but when they reach the end your swipe keeps scrolling the page.
  • ⬆ Top button: appears when you are far down a page and takes you back to the top. Switching tabs from far down also brings the new tab into view.
  • 🍎 Quick Feed: tap a fruit in the Bag to open one panel with your fruits, your units and the Feed buttons together. It stays open while you feed, so no more scrolling up and down.
  • Switch fruits at the top, search or sort your units, and see each unit’s level and EXP bar.
  • Feed 1, Feed 10 and Feed max (up to level 100 or until the fruit runs out) show what you will get before you tap, for example “→ Lv 24”.
  • When a fruit runs out, Quick Feed switches to your next fruit. When a unit reaches level 100, it moves on to the next unit.
  • 🍎 Feed on unit cards: in the Units tab, every unit below level 100 has a Feed button that opens Quick Feed with that unit already chosen.
